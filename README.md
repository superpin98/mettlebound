## About the Game

You drop into the dark. No map. No mercy. No way but down.

**Mettlebound** is a 3D dungeon crawler roguelite that fuses the addictive item chase of *Path of Exile*, the strategic depth of *Slay the Spire*, and the brutal satisfaction of *Darkest Dungeon* — wrapped in a sleek low-poly aesthetic that runs straight in your browser, no install required.

Every floor is procedurally generated. Every enemy encounter is a tactical puzzle fought in a dedicated isometric combat arena. Every piece of loot you pick up has randomized stats, a rarity tier, and the potential to become the cornerstone of a build no one has ever tried before.

You choose one of five distinct classes and push as deep as you can. When you die — and you will die — the run ends permanently. But the dungeon remembers. Permanent progression carries across runs, and each death teaches you something the next adventurer will use to go further.

How deep can you go before the abyss swallows you whole?

## Features

- **Procedurally generated infinite dungeons** — no two runs share the same floor layout, room sequence, or enemy composition
- **Five playable classes with completely unbound build potential** — Warrior, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, and the wild-card Wanderer; your stats, your rules
- **A loot system built for obsession** — five rarity tiers, randomized item stats, set bonuses, and unique effects on Epic and Legendary gear that completely reshape how you play
- **Turn-based tactical combat** — every fight is a decision tree, not a reflex test; position yourself well and you might land the first blow before the enemy even reacts
- **Surprise attack mechanics** — approach enemies from the right angle and steal the first two turns; smart exploration is rewarded, not just brute strength
- **Full environmental interaction in combat** — use the room itself as a weapon; hazards, objects, and the dungeon's own traps are yours to exploit
- **Permadeath that means something** — no mid-run saves, no second chances; the pressure is real, which makes every floor cleared feel earned
- **Permanent metaprogression between runs** — death is not the end; a currency system lets you unlock lasting upgrades that compound across every future run
- **Ten unique enemy types** — each with distinct behavior, special abilities, and tactical counters you'll need to learn the hard way
- **Dark fantasy aesthetic** — a world soaked in shadow, low-poly geometry with carefully crafted lighting, and a tone that takes the descent seriously
- **Runs entirely in the browser** — no downloads, no launchers, no friction
